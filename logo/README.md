# Stacklane brand

The Stacklane symbol shows four rack units in one chassis, joined by a single path that runs from top to bottom.

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `stacklane-horizontal.svg` | Primary logo on light backgrounds |
| `stacklane-horizontal-reversed.svg` | Primary logo on dark or indigo backgrounds |
| `stacklane-horizontal-black.svg` | One-colour print, engraving, fax |
| `stacklane-stacked.svg` | Narrow spaces, square formats |
| `stacklane-symbol.svg` | Symbol on its own, 32 px and up |
| `stacklane-symbol-small.svg` | Symbol below 32 px, with wider slots |
| `stacklane-symbol-black.svg`, `stacklane-symbol-white.svg` | One-colour symbol |
| `stacklane-wordmark.svg` | Wordmark without the symbol |
| `icons/` | Favicon, touch icon, PWA icons, app icon and web manifest |

All artwork is outlined. No fonts are needed to use it.

## Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indigo | `#4F46E5` | 79 70 229 | Symbol, accents |
| Ink | `#0F172A` | 15 23 42 | Wordmark, text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | Reversed logo |

White on indigo has a contrast ratio of 6.3:1.

## Typography

The wordmark is set in Montserrat SemiBold (SIL Open Font License) with tracking reduced by 12 units and converted to outlines.

## Clear space and size

- **Clear space:** keep at least the height of one rack unit free on every side of the logo. That is 3/16 of the symbol's height.
- **Symbol:** at least 16 px. Below 32 px, use `stacklane-symbol-small.svg`.
- **Horizontal logo:** at least 96 px wide on screen and 25 mm in print.

## Do not

- rotate, mirror, skew or stretch the logo
- change the number of units or the direction of the slots
- recolour the symbol and the wordmark in two different accent colours
- add shadows, outlines, gradients or effects
- place the indigo symbol on a busy photo or on a background with low contrast
- reset the wordmark in another typeface
