# v1.1.0.0: VPS and web hosting

<ReleaseMeta />

[Lees dit in het Nederlands](/releases/v1.1.0.0)

With this version you sell more than game servers. Customers order a VPS or web hosting in the panel, and after payment the service is set up on its own.

## VPS on Proxmox VE

Stacklane creates servers on your own Proxmox cluster, from a cloud-init template and with a free IP address from the range you set. Customers power their server on and off, take snapshots, set firewall rules and add SSH keys.

## Web hosting on DirectAdmin or Plesk

Customers choose a package and enter the domain of their website. After payment Stacklane creates the account, and one click takes them into DirectAdmin or Plesk.

## Ordering

Packages can now be ordered in the panel itself. The customer chooses how often to pay and gets an invoice right away. If an order stays unpaid for 14 days, it expires.

## Not yet

cPanel, other VPS platforms and domains are not in this version. They are on the [roadmap](/#roadmap).
