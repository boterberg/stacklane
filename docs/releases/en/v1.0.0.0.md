# v1.0.0.0: the first version

[Lees dit in het Nederlands](/releases/v1.0.0.0)

This is where Stacklane starts: a customer panel where customers manage their services, pay their invoices and ask questions, and where your team handles everything from one admin area.

## Billing

Every subscription has an expiry date. A few days before it, Stacklane creates the renewal invoice. If the customer saved a payment method, the amount is charged right away; otherwise the customer gets a notification and an email. If an invoice stays unpaid too long, the services are suspended, and they come back after payment. Every invoice can be downloaded as a PDF.

## Game servers

Game servers come from Pterodactyl, but the customer stays in Stacklane: console, files, backups and power controls are all in the panel.

## Account and security

Customers manage their details and address, choose a default payment method and turn on two-factor authentication. For scripts they create an API token that can only do what they tick.

## Moving over

With `stacklane import` you bring customers and services over from WHMCS, Paymenter or Pterodactyl. You can safely run an import more than once.

## Installing and updating

Stacklane ships as a Debian package from the repository on stacklane.io. `sudo stacklane setup` prepares a new server and `sudo stacklane update` backs up the database before it updates. The documentation explains how to add the repository; it is in Dutch for now.

## Not yet

VPS, domains and web hosting are not in this version. They are on the [roadmap](/#roadmap).
