# v1.2.0.0: More platforms and domains

<ReleaseMeta />

[Lees dit in het Nederlands](/releases/v1.2.0.0)

This is the biggest update so far. It brings three items from the roadmap: more VPS platforms, domains and cPanel.

## VPS on OpenStack and VirtFusion

Next to Proxmox VE you can now connect an OpenStack project or a VirtFusion installation. Per product you choose the flavor or the VirtFusion package. With VirtFusion the customer manages their server in the VirtFusion panel, and Stacklane logs them in there with one click. See [VPS](/docs/guides/vps).

## Everything around a server

Customers order volumes and floating IPs and move them between their servers. They create private networks, put firewalls on their whole account, import an ISO and boot from it. OpenStack has a rescue mode. A server can be rebuilt with another operating system, and with a PowerDNS connection customers set the reverse DNS of their addresses themselves.

A VPS environment can also be shared. You invite someone and choose per part what they may do: only view, open the console, rebuild or manage users.

## Domains

With an Openprovider account customers search for a domain, register or transfer it, and manage the name servers, the auth code and the transfer lock. You set prices per extension. See [Domains](/docs/guides/domains).

## cPanel and VAT

Web hosting now also runs on cPanel & WHM. Billing charges VAT per customer country, with OSS and reverse charge, and gives you a quarterly overview as CSV.

## Security

Since the previous version Stacklane was reviewed twice from start to finish. Everything that came out of it is fixed, also in the parts that already existed. API tokens expire when your password changes, and the new parts that take over a server, such as rescue and mounting an ISO, only work from the panel. Cancelling, renewing and suspending no longer get in each other's way. An account that someone created on your email address is cleared when you recover your password.

## Updating

Update with `sudo stacklane update`. It makes a backup of your database first. The new migrations run on their own at startup.
