# v1.3.0.0: Made to fit your business

<ReleaseMeta />

[Lees dit in het Nederlands](/releases/v1.3.0.0)

After 1.2 we showed Stacklane to hosters and asked what they missed. This version is their list. Almost everything below came from those conversations.

## Your branding

Under **Admin → Settings → Branding** you set your own name, logo, favicon and accent colour. Without an accent colour the panel stays as it was. With one, the main buttons and the active menu item take your colour. See [Branding](/docs/guides/branding).

## Support that keeps customers informed

When support replies to a ticket, the customer gets a notification and an email with the reply. Tickets waiting for the customer close after a number of days you choose. Under **Admin → Mail log** you see whether each email really went out.

You fill the new help centre under **Admin → Knowledge base**. Customers search it under **Support → Help centre**, and can still open a ticket from any article. See [Tickets](/docs/guides/tickets#helpcentrum).

## An overview for admins

**Admin → Customers** shows every customer with subscriptions, services and unpaid invoices. **Admin → Statistics** shows revenue per month, recurring revenue and how many customers you have.

## Billing and your own website

If you do not invoice in euro, choose your currency in the settings. Existing invoices keep their own currency. With `GET /api/public/catalog` you put your packages and domain prices on your own website, with an order link that takes the customer straight to the right package. See [API](/docs/guides/api#je-pakketten-op-je-eigen-website).

## Game servers

Every game server now has an **Activity** tab. It shows who started or stopped the server, changed files or made a backup.

## Updating

Update from **Admin → System** or with `sudo stacklane update`. The database gets a few new tables; that happens by itself. Everything you need to choose yourself starts switched off: tickets do not close by themselves and the branding stays Stacklane's until you set it.
