# v1.2.0.1: A full demo

<ReleaseMeta />

[Lees dit in het Nederlands](/releases/v1.2.0.1)

A small update for the [demo](https://demo.stacklane.io/login). After 1.2.0.0, logging in there sometimes failed because `PUBLIC_URL` was not set on the demo. That is fixed, and Stacklane now warns at startup when the setting is missing.

The demo now also shows what came with 1.2. The demo customer has two servers with graphs, snapshots and firewall rules, a volume, a floating IP, a private network, ISOs, SSH keys, two domains and a colleague with shared access. The demo does not really carry out actions on the servers.

Nothing changes for your own installation, except the warning when `PUBLIC_URL` is missing.
