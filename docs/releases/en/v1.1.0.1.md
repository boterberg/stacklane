A small update for the [demo](https://demo.stacklane.io/login). It was empty: the shop had no packages and the demo customer had no services.

As a customer you can now choose from three VPS packages, three web hosting packages and three game servers. Ordering is switched off in the demo, but you can see how it works. The demo customer Jan Jansen has a VPS, a Minecraft server and his website, with their subscriptions and invoices.

Nothing changes for your own installation.
